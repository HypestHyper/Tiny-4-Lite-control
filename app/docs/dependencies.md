# Dependencies

Generated by `tiny4lite-control deps`. A setting is switched off, with the reason shown in its row, while its condition is not met. When the camera has not reported the setting it depends on, nothing is blocked.

## Exposure

| Setting | Needs | Shown when blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Metering priority (`obsbot:auto_exposure_mode`) | Exposure mode is Auto | Set Exposure mode to Auto to choose a metering priority. |
| Exposure compensation (`obsbot:exposure_compensation`) | Exposure mode is Auto | Set Exposure mode to Auto to use exposure compensation. |
| Shutter speed (`v4l2:exposure_time_absolute`) | Exposure mode is Manual | Set Exposure mode to Manual to choose a shutter speed. |

## White balance

| Setting | Needs | Shown when blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Colour temperature (`v4l2:white_balance_temperature`) | Auto white balance is off | Turn off Auto white balance to set the colour temperature. |

## Focus

| Setting | Needs | Shown when blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Autofocus priority (`obsbot:focus_mode`) | Autofocus is on | Turn on Autofocus to choose its priority. |
| Manual focus (`v4l2:focus_absolute`) | Autofocus is off | Turn off Autofocus to focus by hand. |

## Zoom and field of view

| Setting | Needs | Shown when blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Zoom (`v4l2:zoom_absolute`) | Tracking mode is No tracking or Normal | Zoom works with tracking off or on Normal. Change Tracking mode to use it. |
| Field of view (`obsbot:fov`) | Tracking mode is No tracking or Normal | Zoom works with tracking off or on Normal. Change Tracking mode to use it. |

## Microphone

| Setting | Needs | Shown when blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Volume (`obsbot:mic_volume`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |
| Listen while asleep (`obsbot:mic_during_sleep`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |
| Auto gain (`obsbot:auto_gain`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |
| Noise reduction (`obsbot:noise_reduction`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |
| Pickup distance (`obsbot:radio_distance`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |
| Pop filter (`obsbot:pop_filter`) | Disable microphone is off | Turn the microphone on to change this. |

## Every numeric setting

A control is also switched off when the camera driver reports it as inactive, or reports a value outside its own range. Sliders take their minimum, maximum and step from the driver; Reset returns to the driver's default (for the colour balance, hue and exposure compensation that is the middle of the range).
