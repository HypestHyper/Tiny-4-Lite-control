Fixes for presets, messages and the window layout.

- Presets no longer fail with "Some settings were not applied". They no longer save or change the microphone switch, Listen while asleep, the capture mode or the camera's sleep timer, and never turn the Studio camera on or off.
- Microphone settings in a preset are only sent while the microphone is on, and the Studio camera's LUT only while the Studio camera is on. Otherwise they are left out with a note, and the rest of the preset still applies.
- A preset that does not apply fully names the settings that did not, in the same words from the Presets tab and the joystick buttons.
- Messages at the bottom stay longer when there is more to read, stay open while the pointer is on them, and have a close button.
- The page no longer shrinks or shows a thick bar at the bottom when the window is about as narrow as the page.

Update by putting the new AppImage in `~/Applications` and opening it. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/HypestHyper/Tiny-4-Lite-control/blob/main/app/CHANGELOG.md).
