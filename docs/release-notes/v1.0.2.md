Fixes from a full review of the app.

- Importing settings restarts the app as it says, also from the AppImage.
- The microphone's settings can only be changed while the microphone is on, and nothing is sent to it while it is off.
- Settings the camera refuses are reported as not changed instead of passing as done.
- The window and the background service no longer write over each other's settings.
- The background service no longer resets the capture mode over and over for cameras that report 29.97 fps.
- Quick controls that had the microphone switch keep it; a replugged camera gets its chosen capture mode back.
- The ±1° steps read the camera's position afresh when the one on screen is old.
- Importing a file that is not this app's settings is refused instead of resetting everything.
- Smaller fixes: messages closed with their close button no longer hold the next ones open, and a preset that does not apply fully names each setting once.

Update by putting the new AppImage in `~/Applications` and opening it. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/HypestHyper/Tiny-4-Lite-control/blob/main/app/CHANGELOG.md).
